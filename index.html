HISTORICAL_DEDICATION // BIOMEDICAL_LEGACY

In Recognition of Henrietta Lacks

The HeLa MCP Ecosystem is named in respectful recognition of Henrietta Lacks (1920–1951) and the immortal scientific legacy of HeLa cells, which transformed biomedical research across the twentieth century. In this software architecture, the HeLa designation represents modular specialization, cellular longevity, and deterministic replicability.
