In Respectful Recognition of Henrietta Lacks

The HeLa MCP Ecosystem is named in honor and respectful recognition of Henrietta Lacks (1920–1951) and the immortal scientific legacy of HeLa cells, which transformed global biomedical research, vaccine development, and cellular biology.

In this architecture, HeLa serves as an architectural cellular metaphor: representing modular specialization, deterministic replicability, and immortal longevity in autonomous software systems.
